Frequently Asked Questions

Spanish and Catalan lessons: common questions.

Should I learn Spanish before moving to Spain?

Starting before you arrive can give you the language you need for introductions, transportation, shopping, appointments, and housing.

How much Spanish do I need to live in Spain?

There is no required level. Even basic Spanish can make daily life easier and help you feel more independent.

Can I learn Spanish online before moving to Spain?

Yes. You can begin anywhere in the world and continue with the same teacher after you arrive.

Are these online Spanish lessons suitable for Americans living in Spain?

Yes. Lessons are designed for Americans and other English-speaking expats, newcomers, and international residents. Students learning from outside Spain are equally welcome.

Should I learn Spanish or Catalan in Barcelona?

Spanish is useful throughout Spain. Catalan can help you connect more deeply with everyday life and culture in Barcelona and Catalonia. I can help you decide where to begin.

Do I need Catalan to live in Barcelona?

Not always. However, learning Catalan can help you understand local institutions, media, culture, and conversations—and feel more connected to the community.

Is Catalan difficult for English speakers?

Catalan becomes much clearer when its patterns are explained in relation to English and Spanish. Lessons focus on understanding and communication, not memorization alone.

Why learn Catalan if I already speak Spanish?

Catalan gives you access to another important part of life in Catalonia and helps you communicate with people in the language they may use most naturally.

Do you offer private and small-group lessons?

Yes. Choose private lessons or learn with a partner, friends, family, or a small group with similar goals.

Is the first lesson free?

Yes. We will discuss your goals, identify your current level, and decide on the best approach for you.
