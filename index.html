Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about learning Spanish or Catalan online.

Still deciding where to begin? Send me your question.

Should I learn Spanish before moving to Spain?

Starting before you arrive can help you build confidence without the pressure of navigating everything at once. Your lessons can focus on introductions, transportation, shopping, appointments, housing, and other situations you are likely to encounter while settling in Spain.

How much Spanish do I need to live in Spain?

There is no single required level. Even a practical beginner foundation can make everyday life easier. We will create a learning plan based on where you live, your work, your social life, and the situations in which you expect to use Spanish.

Can I learn Spanish online before moving to Spain?

Yes. Because all lessons are online, you can begin before your move and continue with the same teacher after you arrive. Your classes can evolve alongside your experience of living in Spain.

Are these Spanish classes designed for Americans living in Spain?

Yes, but not exclusively. Classes are especially well suited to Americans and other English speakers moving to or living in Spain. International students who simply want to learn Spanish online are equally welcome.

Should I learn Spanish or Catalan in Barcelona?

Spanish is useful throughout Spain, while Catalan plays an important role in everyday life and culture in Barcelona and the rest of Catalonia. Many newcomers begin with Spanish and add Catalan according to their location, interests, and goals. I can help you decide which language to prioritize.

Do I need Catalan to live in Barcelona?

You can manage many situations in Barcelona without speaking Catalan. However, learning it can help you understand local institutions, media, signs, cultural references, and conversations—and participate more fully in the community.

Is Catalan difficult for English speakers?

Every language presents challenges, but Catalan contains patterns that become clearer when explained in relation to English and Spanish. Lessons focus on practical communication and understanding rather than memorization alone.

Why learn Catalan if I already speak Spanish?

Speaking Catalan gives you access to another important part of life in Catalonia. It can help you connect with local culture, understand more of what you hear and read, and communicate with people in the language they may use most naturally.

Do you offer private and group online lessons?

Yes. You can choose personalized one-on-one lessons or small-group classes. Groups can be formed among friends, partners, family members, or students with compatible levels and goals.

Is the first lesson free?

Yes. Your first online lesson is free. We will discuss your goals, identify your current level, and begin exploring the most useful learning approach for you.
